JEON JUNGKOOK

Singer • Performer • Artist

About Jungkook

Jeon Jungkook is a South Korean singer and performer. He is a member of the globally recognized group BTS. He is known for his vocals, performances, and versatility. He has also developed a successful career as a solo artist.

IMPORTANT
LIFE EVENTS

  1. Born on September 1, 1997, in Busan, South Korea.
  2. Joined Big Hit Entertainment as a trainee in 2011.
  3. Debuted as a member of BTS in 2013.
  4. Released his first solo album "Golden" in 2023.
  5. Continued his career as a solo artist and member of BTS.

KEY CONTRIBUTIONS

  • Contributed to the global success of BTS.
  • Built a successful career as a solo artist.
  • Released his debut solo album "Golden" in 2023.
  • Known for his vocals, dancing, and stage performances.
  • Reached audiences worldwide through his music.
